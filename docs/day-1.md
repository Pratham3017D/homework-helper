# Homework Helper — Day 1 Progress

## Project

**Homework Helper for Parents**

### Core Promise

> Understand the topic in 2 minutes. Help your child in 10.

The product is designed to help parents understand their child's homework topic well enough to explain it to them, rather than simply giving the child an answer.

---

# 1. Project Goal

The eventual product flow is:

Parent enters homework topic
        ↓
Selects grade + subject
        ↓
AI creates learning guide
        ↓
Parent briefing
        ↓
Teaching script
        ↓
Common mistakes
        ↓
5 practice problems
        ↓
Hints
        ↓
Answers + solutions
        ↓
Printable worksheet
